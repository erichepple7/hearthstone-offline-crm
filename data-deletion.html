Social Account Data Deletion

To remove a connected social account and its authorization data:

  1. Open Marketing Center in Eric Hepple CRM.
  2. Select Manage connections.
  3. Select Disconnect beside Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok.
  4. Confirm the request. The CRM will revoke or remove the stored connection credentials and stop future publishing.

If the CRM connection screen is unavailable, email erichepple7@gmail.com with the platform and connected account name. The request will be completed as soon as reasonably possible.

Platform controls

You may also revoke access directly from the connected platform’s security or authorized-app settings. Revoking platform access prevents additional publishing even before the CRM’s local status refreshes.

Marketing campaign records that do not contain authorization credentials may remain in the private CRM unless separately deleted by the owner.
