Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Eric Hepple CRM is a private real-estate customer relationship and marketing tool operated by Eric Hepple. This policy explains how the CRM handles information when an authorized social-media account is connected.

Information used

  • Basic account identity, such as the connected account name and platform identifier.
  • Authorization tokens supplied by Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok.
  • Marketing content that the account owner selects for publishing, including captions, photos, videos, scheduling information, and resulting post identifiers.
  • Technical status information needed to confirm whether a publishing request succeeded or failed.

How information is used

Information is used only to prepare, review, schedule, publish, and track marketing content requested by the CRM owner. The CRM does not sell personal information or use connected social accounts for unrelated advertising.

Publishing controls

Marketing content is not published until the CRM owner reviews the campaign and gives final approval. Account passwords are never requested or stored by the CRM. Authorization is handled by each platform’s official sign-in process.

Storage and security

Connection credentials are kept in restricted server-side storage and are not placed in public website files. Access is limited to the authorized CRM owner. Credentials are removed when an account is disconnected or a deletion request is completed.

Sharing

Selected marketing content is shared only with the social platforms chosen for that campaign. Service providers may process limited technical information when necessary to host the CRM and complete an authorized publishing request.

Retention and deletion

Connection information is retained while an account remains connected. The owner may disconnect an account in Marketing Center or follow the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to erichepple7@gmail.com.
